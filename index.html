About us

Who we are

Aqsa Al Bayan Makhrata (شركة اقصى البيان للخراطه) provides engine reconditioning and precision machining for automotive and industrial engine components.

Our workshop in the Oraifi Industrial District, Al Jubail is equipped with specialized machines for crankshaft grinding, cylinder and block boring, honing, line boring, connecting rod boring and hydraulic pressing. We focus on accurate machining, proper component preparation and reliable workshop service.

Seven machines. Eight services. Automotive and industrial work, all under one roof.

Our mission

To provide accurate, dependable engine reconditioning and precision machining that prepares engine components for reliable performance, through specialized equipment, careful measurement and honest, professional workshop service.

Our vision

To be a trusted name in engine reconditioning and precision machining for automotive and industrial customers, known for accuracy, consistent quality and long-term relationships built on customer-focused service.

How we work

What happens to your part

Five steps from the moment it arrives to the moment it’s ready to rebuild.

  1. 1

    Inspect and measure

    Each part is inspected and measured to confirm condition and suitability.

  2. 2

    Machine

    Grinding, boring or line boring on the right machine for the job.

  3. 3

    Finish

    Honing and fine surface finishing, including cylinder bores.

  4. 4

    Fit and press

    Pressing, fitting and removal of bushes and press-fit parts.

  5. 5

    Rebuild-ready

    Components prepared to the measurements needed for rebuilding.

Good to know. Actual machining dimensions, tolerances, bore ranges and component suitability are confirmed after inspection and measurement of the individual part, and according to the capability of the machine.
